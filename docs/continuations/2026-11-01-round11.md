# Round 11 — new sixth-node tests and stable interval evaluation

**Date:** November 1, 2026. **Scientific status:** no RH proof or disproof. Direction: Christopher Kiser, with substantial AI assistance.

## New finding and scope

Round 10 closed coefficient-only searches at five fixed nodes. Round 11 changes actual kernel data by adding a sixth node, one at a time, while retaining the same sixteen-pair deflation. Four separate extensions have strictly positive interval Schur bounds:

| New node | Lower bound | Upper bound |
|---|---:|---:|
| 50+i/10 | 4.4571320e-8 | 4.4571333e-8 |
| 60+i/4 | 2.3863328e-5 | 2.3863331e-5 |
| 80+i/5 | 2.2044767876 | 2.2044767890 |
| 100+i/10 | 1.5494288324 | 1.5494288334 |

These are Schur complements, not smallest eigenvalues. Positive core plus positive Schur complement establishes positivity for every vector in each specified six-node configuration. It does **not** establish positivity of a nine-node matrix containing all four new points together, a continuous domain of node locations, arbitrary further deflation, or global RH.

For the block B=[[A,b],[b*,d]], a rational candidate y gives q=(-y,1)*B(-y,1) and residual r=Ay-b. Using the inherited bound A>=mu I,

$$q-\|r\|^2/\mu\le d-b^*A^{-1}b\le q.$$

All uncertainties in A,b,d and the residual are enclosed. The full xi logarithmic derivative is evaluated; the unknown zero tail is not discarded.

## Collision normalization: tiny scores are not an approaching disproof

At an old node a, subtracting the core interpolation gives an analytic/anti-analytic residual kernel S with S(a,w)=S(z,a)=0. Consequently, locally,

$$S(z,w)=(z-a)(\overline w-\overline a)H(z,w).$$

The added-node Schur score thus vanishes at least quadratically as z approaches a, without any RH assumption. Divided-difference normalization by |z-a|^2 preserves its sign for nonzero separation. H can have either sign. This is a written analytic deduction with exact controls, not a new RH criterion or a proof that close nodes are always positive.

Four tests at a=14.1+i/10 with real separations 1e-2 through 1e-5 remained **INCONCLUSIVE** under interval arithmetic. Ordinary high-precision values decrease from about 2.24e-22 to 1.86e-28; the normalized values approach a derivative-based numerical estimate 1.86234387319622e-18. Neither those tiny signs nor that limit is interval-certified. Dividing an uncertain interval by a small number does not improve its accuracy.

Exact synthetic controls give normalized limits of opposite signs, 1/4 and -512/4222619375. They are artificial rational functions, not zeta counterexamples.

## Evaluator improvement and preserved failure

The initial new100 attempt was inconclusive because the fixed-grid evaluator rounded a tiny power of N before multiplying it by a huge rising factorial. The rounding remained conservative, but the interval widened drastically. The initial result is preserved rather than relabeled as a negative witness.

A separate balanced implementation propagates p_l=(s)_l/N^l and its derivative using

$$p_{l+1}=p_l(s+l)/N,\qquad p'_{l+1}=[p'_l(s+l)+p_l]/N.$$

This is algebraically the same Euler–Maclaurin formula with the same explicit remainder bounds. No bound was weakened and the original evaluator is preserved unchanged. At s=0.6-100i, the real zeta enclosure width fell from approximately 6.7222e-4 to 6.5861e-63 at the same truncation and grid settings. Independent numerical values lie inside both enclosures. The improvement is in evaluation order, not an analytic RH advance; the old enclosure was loose, not invalid.

## Verification record and trust boundary

Five connected Wolfram batches completed. Sixteen known zero pairs were freshly authenticated by local isolation and symmetry; these are not newly discovered zeros. Independent checks used 100-digit mpmath for 16 root-containment comparisons, 88 new matrix real-component comparisons, eight Schur-containment comparisons and 32 old/new special-function component comparisons. Eighteen symbolic identities, twelve exact rational Schur controls, and three sign controls passed.

The old five-node core enclosure is inherited and its exact positivity certificate was replayed. New columns and diagonals were evaluated afresh. The custom evaluator and analytic remainder derivations still need external expert review. No Arb, Lean, hosted CI success, or new zero-free range is claimed. ## Research decision

Do not repeat coefficient-only scans in the four separately excluded six-node configurations. Do not interpret raw near-collision scores as discovery signals. A rigorous derivative or divided-difference evaluator is needed to decide the close-node tests; their positive numerical limit is not promoted to a theorem. Different unexcluded kernel data or an independently justified arithmetic estimate remains necessary.

All historical notes are preserved. Full Haglund k=2/k=3 verification, the marked-inverse estimate, and historical provenance retain their unresolved statuses. No literature-priority claim is made.
