# Round 9 — a uniform exclusion for a remote-root filter family

**Date:** November 1, 2026. **Status:** conditional computational search exclusion; no RH proof or disproof. Research direction: Christopher Kiser, with substantial AI assistance.

## Finding

The preceding exploration used five fixed upper-half-plane nodes and the genuine xi logarithmic-derivative Pick matrix, with sixteen authenticated real-zero pairs removed. One proposed filter numerator was

$$P_G(w)=(w^2-g^2)(w^2-G^2),$$

where g is an authenticated ordinate near 69.546401711174 and G is a freely chosen filter parameter, not an observed zeta-zero height. Testing isolated values of G is unnecessary: the entire family lies in one two-dimensional coefficient subspace.

Set P0(w)=w^2-g^2 and P1(w)=-w^2 P0(w). The exact identity P_G=-G^2(P0+P1/G^2), with the G=0 case handled separately, reduces the problem to the Hermitian compression S=U* R U. Here R is the fixed genuine deflated matrix and U contains the two residue vectors associated with P0 and P1. The full infinite xi contribution remains in R; this is not a finite-zero approximation.

## Result and scope

An independent Python Fraction interval replay encloses the compression with a strictly positive leading diagonal and determinant greater than 1.1998338053e-12. It yields

$$v^*R v / \|v\|^2 > 2.6\times10^{-18}$$

for every nonzero v in this specified two-dimensional complex subspace. Thus every finite real or complex G in this family is excluded as a negative witness, conditional on the inherited matrix and root enclosures.

This is not a statement about all vectors in the five-dimensional matrix, different nodes, different deflations, or global RH. It does not exclude off-line zeta zeros. The result is a method-specific obstruction, not evidence that RH has been proved.
