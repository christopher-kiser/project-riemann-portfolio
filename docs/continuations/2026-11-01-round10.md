# Round 10 — whole-space exclusion of the fixed five-node search

**Date:** November 1, 2026. **Classification:** conditional exact computational exclusion. **No RH proof or disproof.** Direction: Christopher Kiser, with substantial AI assistance.

## Main finding

Round 9 excluded a two-dimensional filter family. The stronger Round 10 calculation excludes **every nonzero complex vector in the entire fixed five-node space**, conditional on the inherited interval enclosures:

$$v^*R_{16}v/\|v\|^2 > 1.5\times10^{-14}.$$

The five nodes are 14+i/10, 14.1+i/10, 21+i/5, 30+i/3 and 40+i/2. R16 is the genuine xi Pick matrix after the same sixteen authenticated real-zero pair removals used in Round 9. The infinite contribution is not discarded.

This includes every proper rational-filter numerator of degree at most four at these nodes, irrespective of its root locations or coefficients. It also covers removing fewer of the same real zeros, because their contributions are positive semidefinite. It does not cover additional removals, different nodes, larger matrices or derivative data. Entry-uncertainty coverage is not a continuous-node result.

## Why the sign is certified

The checker builds the exact rational midpoint M of the supplied Hermitian intervals, proves M positive by exact LDL factorization, and checks its exact inverse on both sides. Since the eigenvalues are positive, lambda_min(M)>=1/trace(M^-1). The symmetric maximum row sum of entry-error bounds controls the spectral error.

## Research decision

Close coefficient and root-location optimization at these fixed nodes with the same or fewer sixteen-pair removals. The whole-matrix test should have preceded further family scans; Round 10 supersedes their research priority while preserving the earlier reports.

A genuinely new node can be screened by a Schur complement with a solver-residual error bound. For a positive core A>=mu I, rational y, r=Ay-b and q=(-y,1)*B(-y,1), the true Schur complement lies between q-||r||^2/mu and q. This standard identity was tested exactly on synthetic controls; no genuine sixth-node interval result is claimed here.

The next candidate must change actual kernel data or provide a new analytic estimate. This exclusion does not prove or disprove RH. Haglund, marked-inverse and provenance statuses remain unchanged.
