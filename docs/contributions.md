# Contribution and attribution statement

**Research lead: Christopher Kiser.** Project Riemann is an independent, AI-assisted research initiative. It is not presented as institutional employment, a peer-reviewed RH result, or work written entirely without AI assistance.

## My role

I initiated and directed the project, selected questions to pursue, set verification and preservation requirements, and asked for independent challenges to proposed arguments. The recorded research includes my observation about repeatedly selecting primes at prime-numbered positions and studying the skipped integers, prime-valued gaps, runs and further nested patterns. That observation led to the nested-prime investigations documented here.

My contribution includes maintaining the research agenda, demanding exact hypotheses and reproducible evidence, requiring failures to remain visible, and deciding that an attractive idea should not be promoted without the missing proof. These are documented project responsibilities, not a claim that I independently authored or mastered every technique in the repository.
