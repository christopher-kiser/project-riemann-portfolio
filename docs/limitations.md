# Limitations, corrections and stopped approaches

- RH is open; complete Haglund k=2/k=3 verification remains unverified.
- The strengthened marked inverse moment remains blocked by an unproved analytic estimate. Its negative initialization margin does not disprove the estimate.
- Raw positive moments are not the factorial-normalized Xi Jensen coefficients.
- Möbius is multiplicative on coprime inputs, **not completely multiplicative**: μ(p²)=0 but μ(p)²=1. Older wording in some archived notes must be read with this correction.
- A finite Euler-product logarithm is a consistently chosen sum of local logarithms; it is not necessarily the principal logarithm of the product everywhere.
- Any first-harmonic pole-free criterion must handle the known pole at s=1 separately.
- Positive kernels, heat flow, finite coherence, random-phase theorems, density-one statements and fixed finite prime filters do not by themselves exclude every actual off-line zero.
- Artificial countermodels test an inference under their stated properties. They are not genuine zeta counterexamples.
- The gap-clock note remains supplementary and not adopted. Its external prime-gap inputs were not independently checked in the records campaign.
- Historical provenance remains `BLOCKED_UNWAIVED`; no capture reference was removed or checker weakened to change that status.
- Literature priority is not established. “Project-specific investigation” does not mean nobody previously explored the mathematics.

Archived notes preserve historical statements and may contain obsolete runtime descriptions, paths replaced by `LOCAL_ARCHIVE`, or claims awaiting independent review. The current status, this correction sheet and the classified registry govern interpretation. Original notes are not silently rewritten to conceal a change of conclusion.
