# Research maintenance and publication policy

Authorized by Christopher Kiser on November 1, 2026. Applies to work performed during active research sessions; it is not a scheduled or background service.

## Repository roles

This portfolio is the canonical public research index and status summary. The private `project-riemann-verifier` repository holds unreleased implementation, detailed continuation notes and evidence. The earlier public `project-riemann-research` overview points readers to the current status. Do not change repository visibility or publish private code merely to synchronize summaries.

## After each completed research batch

Record a dated note, exact claim and domain, prerequisites, evidence classification, tests actually run, negative/inconclusive results, source references, trust limitations and the next decision. Record the commit and verify the saved content before describing it as synchronized. Add new entries to the continuation registry without rewriting historical claims as if they were newly proved.

Classifications must distinguish exact algebra, inherited conditional certificates, numerical diagnostics, synthetic controls, published external results and unresolved conjectures. A checksum proves byte identity, not a theorem. A failed sufficient test is inconclusive, not a negative witness. A positive finite calculation is not a proof of an infinite assertion.

## Preservation and corrections

Keep old notes and manifests accessible. Correct errors with a dated correction or superseding entry. Update current-file hashes when tracked files change, while retaining the previous manifest. Do not weaken a checker, remove provenance failures or silently reset a closed research direction to obtain a passing status.

The October 20 registry remains a 76-record snapshot; subsequent findings belong in `data/continuation-claims.csv` until deliberately reconciled. Its verifier's fixed counts must not be silently changed to manufacture a new total.

## Verification and disclosure

State when input enclosures were inherited rather than regenerated. Preserve failures and tool warnings. Do not suppress RH-dependent solver assumptions. Report Lean compilation, hosted CI, third-party replay and expert review only when actually completed. Local passing tests are not substitutes for external review.

Preserve the project's explicit AI-assistance disclosure and do not claim first-in-literature novelty without a separate review. No RH proof or disproof, full Haglund verification or stronger arithmetic estimate is promoted without its own complete evidence.

## Publication boundary

Public summaries may describe reviewed-for-release findings and limitations. Releasing the private implementation or full historical corpus requires a deliberate publication decision. Repository updates do not by themselves authorize paid cloud computation or expensive historical campaigns.
